# Help

**Runs:** on demand ("help", "what can you do?").
**May write:** nothing.

Reply with this card, in English:

```
I'm your work assistant. Things you can say:
• "brief me": today's meetings, what's due in Jira ANL, meeting prep, focus slots, stand-up draft
• "stand-up": Yesterday / Today / Blockers with ticket keys, for you to paste at 09:45
• "what's on my plate": your open ANL issues, due soon and blocked first
• "prep my 11:00" (or any meeting): related tickets, Slack questions, what to bring
• "Friday update": shipped / in progress / next / risks, for your manager
• "find 2 hours for ANL-412", "block focus time this week": a table of focus blocks in your Assistant calendar, booked after your OK
• "remind me Friday at 15:00 about …": a reminder in your Assistant calendar
Automatic:
• Weekdays 08:30: morning briefing with the stand-up draft (in Claude's Scheduled page + a Slack DM to you)
• Fridays 15:30: Friday update draft (Scheduled page + a Slack DM to you)
I never send email, never post in Slack channels or Jira for you, never need your passwords,
never show "confidential" tickets, and never plan after 18:30, at weekends, at lunch, or
meetings on Wednesday.
Monthly: say "tune-up" and we'll adjust things together.
```
