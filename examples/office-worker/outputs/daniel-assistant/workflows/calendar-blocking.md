# Focus blocks and reminders

**Runs:** on demand ("find 2 hours for ANL-412", "block focus time this week", "remind me
Friday at 15:00 about …").
**May write:** events in **Assistant** only, after OK when there are 2 or more (rule 6).
Never moves or deletes Daniel's own events or anything in "Outlook (work)".

## Rules (from SYSTEM.md §6, apply all)
- Busy anyway: meetings in "Outlook (work)"; working hours Mon–Fri 09:00–18:00
- **Protected, never:** weekdays after 18:30; Saturday and Sunday; lunch 14:00–15:00
- No-go days: Wednesday for meetings. Focus blocks on Wednesday are welcome; never place or
  propose a meeting then.
- Quiet hours: 18:30–08:30 and weekends (no blocks and no reminders)
- Daily cap on planned focus: 3 h
- Block length: 30–90 minutes; 10 minutes after a meeting before a block starts

## Steps
1. Read busy times from "Outlook (work)", "Assistant" and the primary calendar (busy/free
   only) for the requested range (default: next 5 working days).
2. List candidate slots that pass every rule. Prefer mornings after the stand-up
   (10:10–12:00) and Wednesdays.
3. Propose a table:

   | # | Day | Time | Title | Calendar | Reminder |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | Thu 23 Oct | 10:10–11:40 | ◆ Focus: ANL-412 cohort filter | Assistant | 10 min |

   Title prefix "◆ ". Never put a confidential issue's title in an event; use "◆ Focus"
   only. If the request can't fit, say so and offer the nearest options outside the rules
   only as a question, never as a booking.
4. After OK, create them, then list what was created. Remind Daniel the blocks don't show
   as busy in Outlook.

## Reminders
One dictated reminder ("remind me Friday at 15:00 to send the update") goes straight in as
"◆ Reminder: …" in **Assistant** with a 10-minute notification, if the time is inside working
hours and not protected. Otherwise ask for a time that is.

## Moving things
"Move my focus block to tomorrow": only events in **Assistant** with prefix "◆ ". Update,
don't delete-and-recreate.
