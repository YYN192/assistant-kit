**With the Lena's-assistant skill**
- **Briefings:** "brief me" or "what's today" gives a rundown of your day. The skill also lists a weekday 08:15 scheduled run.
- **Tasks and homework:** it can add homework or tasks, show what's due, and mark things done. You can paste lecture slides or an email for it to pull tasks from.
- **Inbox triage:** it checks your uni and personal inboxes for anything important. Bank and health emails are only counted as "private, skipped".
- **Weekly planning:** "plan my week" runs a weekly review. The skill also lists a Sunday 17:00 scheduled run.
- **Calendar blocking:** "block 2 hours for…" or "find time to study" looks for slots. It avoids Friday after 18:00, Sunday before 12:00, and 23:00–08:30.
- **Tune-up:** "tune-up" is for when something isn't working, or as a monthly check.

**Rules the skill follows**
- Email is drafts only. I never send, forward, or answer invites.
- Everything is read-only unless a workflow names a write.
- Anything involving two or more calendar events or tasks goes in a table first, and I wait for your OK.
- I ask before deleting anything.
- I never handle passwords, codes, or tokens.

**Connectors:** this is an offline smoke test, so none are available. The skill would need your email, calendar, Canvas, and Todoist connectors for most of the above. In a real run I'd use them for the briefing, inbox triage, task capture, and calendar blocking.
